About

Who is James Hagens?

James Hagens (born November 3, 2006) is an American professional ice hockey center for the Boston Bruins of the National Hockey League. He grew up in Hauppauge, on Long Island, and shoots left.

Hagens came through the U.S. National Team Development Program, where he set a World U-17 Hockey Challenge record with 21 points and was named MVP of the 2024 IIHF World U18 Championship after a tournament-record 22 points.

At Boston College he scored 84 points in 71 games over two seasons. As a sophomore in 2025-26 he led the Eagles with 23 goals and 47 points, won the Hockey East scoring title and the Beanpot MVP, and was named a First-Team All-American and a Hobey Baker top-10 finalist.

The Bruins drafted him seventh overall in 2025 and signed him to a three-year entry-level contract in April 2026. He wears #44 because the Bruins retired #4 for Bobby Orr.
